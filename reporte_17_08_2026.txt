WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
- Standalone Python package (seasonbook). Does not modify AlpacaManager or
  Hereditas. Same science as Hereditas: F of a cria = Malécot coancestry of
  the parents, expanded by topological pedigree depth. Path lists are barn
  narration; scoring uses the recurrence.
- Ingest of 74 AOA lineage markdowns from AlpacaManager/docs/cert_lineage.
  Identity is AR number first, cleaned name second, plus a "X BY Y" alias so
  ALYDAR BY VAL D LSERE merges with registered Alydar. OCR crumbs (lone "a",
  "Pa We Ye od") are dropped so they cannot fake shared founders.
- Live graph: 950 animals, 370 founders, 74 registered (48 dams x 26 sires,
  0 unsexed). Mean F 1.00%. Highest: RR Paloma Picasso = RR Wonder Woman
  at 8.98%. Smokin Waves F = 6.64%.
- Effective size of the nucleus Ne ~ 41.6; founder genome equivalents ~ 26.8.
  Legacy sits in 49/74 pedigrees; Hemingway in 39/74. Presence is not gene share.
- Close-kin audit of all registered dam x sire pairs: 15 BLOCK, 20 CONFIRM.
  Gold: Aftershock x Alydar = 25.20% BLOCK (parent x offspring). Inspiration x
  Shogun = full sibs. Smokin Waves x his four daughters land 26.7-29.5%.
- Year-1 assigner: bottleneck dams first, never BLOCK, minimise F then sire
  mean kinship, capacity 4. Matrix is legal on 48/48 and still sits out —
  lower-MK sires fill capacity first. Three-season rotation prefers unused
  stallions in years 2 and 3.
- Command Center at http://127.0.0.1:8765/ (keys 1-8). Printables:
  SeasonBook.pdf, DoNotBook.pdf (barn wall), FounderCensus.pdf, Rotation.pdf.
- CLI: analyze | plan | audit | explain | why | cover | kinship | horizon |
  book | serve.
- 16 tests green, including parent x offspring = 0.25 regardless of id order
  and the live Aftershock x Alydar gold.
